# Case 20 — Portfolio Capstone

**Industry:** Open · **Difficulty:** SENIOR

## The situation

There's no stakeholder here. This one is for your portfolio.

Pick any one of the earlier cases you've done — or a public dataset you care
about — and turn it into a finished, shareable piece of analysis: the kind of
thing you'd link from a CV or send to a hiring manager.

## What makes it a capstone

The earlier cases hand you a situation. Here you define it. A good capstone:

- **starts from a real question**, not "here's a dataset". Someone should want
  to know the answer.
- **shows the work honestly** — what you found in the data, what you cleaned and
  why, what you decided not to trust.
- **lands on a decision or a recommendation**, not just charts. What should
  someone do differently because of this?
- **is readable by a non-analyst.** Plain language. One clear headline.
- **names its limits.** What would you check with more time or better data?

## What to hand back

- the question, in one sentence
- your approach and the two or three findings that matter
- the recommendation
- where it lives (a notebook, a doc, a dashboard, a repo) and the one-line
  version you'd put in a message

## Submit

Describe your capstone and paste the headline findings below, then send it to
your PM for a final review.
