# How to use this OS

## The one-sentence version

This is not a course you finish, it is a workshop you keep coming back to, and it works best when you loop between reading, doing, and being reviewed rather than trying to complete it linearly.

## The pieces and what each is for

**The Constellation Map.** The skill tree. Every dot is a skill; bright ones are open, dim ones unlock as you clear what comes before. Use it to decide what is next, not to grind top to bottom.

**The Textbook.** Short chapters, one concept each. Read the chapter for a node, then leave. It is a reference, not a novel. Come back when a case or a game exposes a gap.

**The Case Files.** Twenty real scenarios, from a retail sales audit to a SaaS cohort analysis. Each hands you a messy dataset with a story buried in it. This is the point of everything else. A skill you have read about but not used on a case is not yet a skill.

**The Games.** Timed drills. Data Detective (spot the bad row), Chart Critiquer (is this chart honest), Pivot Puzzle (group-by instinct), SQL Dojo (query patterns). Do a few rounds when you have ten minutes. They build the reflexes the cases assume.

**The PM (L_OS COMMS).** Reviews your case submissions and decides whether they are done. It will not accept work that has not actually been done. Use it to check your analysis and to ask where to go when a concept has not landed.

**The Review queue.** Spaced repetition. Concepts you have learned come back for a quick check on a schedule, so they do not fade. Clear it when it has items.

**The Daily Log, Canvas, Cheatcodes, Toolkit.** Support. A place to write one line a day, a scratchpad to think on, a quick SQL and Excel reference, and a guide to what to install so you can practise for real.

## The loop that works

1. Open the map, pick the next bright node.
2. Read its chapter (10-20 minutes).
3. Immediately do the case or game it points at.
4. Send the case to your PM for review. Fix what it flags.
5. Let the milestone fire, the coins land, the node go green.
6. Back to the map. Repeat.

The thing that does not work is reading ten chapters in a row and then trying to do a case. The knowledge has nowhere to attach.

## Progress, honestly

XP is your learning progress and cannot be spent. Coins are cosmetic only, earned from doing real work, spent in the Shop on things that are purely for the vibe. Streaks reward showing up. None of it affects what you learn; it exists because grinding into competence is hard and a bit of feedback helps.

## The move

Today: open the map, pick one bright node, read it, do the thing it points at, send it for review. That is a complete session. Do that most days and in a few months you will be able to take a vague request from a manager and turn it into an answer they can act on. That is the whole target.
